This project examines how neighborhood built-environment characteristics — including access to food, healthcare, green space, and environmental burdens — vary across US census tracts by concentration of essential workers, using 2020 data from the National Neighborhood Data Archive (NaNDA). Tracts are stratified into quintiles of essential worker proportion (EWRKR_PROP) derived from 2016–2020 ACS estimates, with analyses conducted overall and stratified by urbanicity (RUCA codes).
